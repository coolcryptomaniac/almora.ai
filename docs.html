How Almora.ai
is supposed to work.

A readable guide to the product, data model, AI agents, moderation, Firebase architecture and the boundaries that keep a civic AI useful without pretending it is the government.

MissionData trustAI agentsResident reportsFirebaseNews policySports directoryContribute
01 · MISSION

One local intelligence layer.

Almora.ai is designed to connect information that is usually fragmented across government pages, local businesses, transport operators, residents, maps, jobs and community knowledge. The main interface is conversational, but the underlying system should always expose sources, freshness and actions.

AI-first does not mean AI-only.

Important civic, health, wildlife, employment, payment and accusation-related actions retain a human or institutional accountability boundary.

02 · DATA TRUST

Verified-first, not “AI said so.”

Public facts should carry a source and verification time. Private resident reports remain private until moderation. Demo/seed markers must never be visually confused with verified live incidents.

Current public sources

District Almora, Uttarakhand government departments, OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, moderated Firestore collections and explicitly sourced public directories.

04 · REPORTS

Report → moderate → publish.

The resident report flow collects category, broad location and factual description. New reports enter a private queue. Moderators can verify, reject or promote appropriate information to a public collection. This prevents an allegation or mistaken report becoming a “fact” simply because someone submitted it.

05 · FIREBASE

Realtime, managed backend.

Cloud Firestore stores reports and public collections. Firebase Authentication protects moderator actions. Security rules keep candidate profiles private and prevent anonymous users from reading the moderation queue.

The browser Firebase config is not equivalent to an Admin credential. Service-account keys must never be committed to GitHub.

06 · EDITORIAL POLICY

Important news, not outrage bait.

The Almora Now feed prioritises major innovation, policy, jobs, infrastructure, mobility, education, economy, sports and civic updates. Routine crime and low-value incidents are intentionally de-emphasised unless they become major state or national stories or create a material public-safety need.

07 · SPORTS + FITNESS

A living sports layer.

The sports section combines prominent Almora athletes, official sports infrastructure and discoverable local gyms/fitness centres. Private businesses are directory listings rather than government endorsements, and corrections should be easy to submit.

08 · OPEN SOURCE

Help build the town layer.

The repository is public. Useful contributions include verified local data adapters, Hindi/Kumaoni UX, accessibility, testing, jobs, transport, sports, wildlife/farming workflows and privacy improvements.
