Autonomous mobility is becoming a room.

WEDGE

Luxury fleet before mass consumer.

Executive shuttles, premium travel, hotels, resorts and events make the value of a self-preparing cabin economically legible before the full consumer product exists.

MOAT

The in-cabin physical-intelligence dataset.

Every service action teaches the system about manipulation, preferences, human proximity, cabin constraints and task recovery in a uniquely intimate physical environment.

CAPITAL

Milestone-gated, not fantasy-gated.

The initial raise should fund a working living-cabin demonstrator, constrained copilot, teleoperation stack and at least one premium fleet pilot—not a clean-sheet mass-market car factory.

FUNDING NARRATIVE

Target: prove the category, then scale the platform.

Illustrative capital plan: $5M prototype/pilot floor; subsequent larger round only after validated demand, safety and repeatable task completion. $100M is a scale ambition, not a claimed current round.
