SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Four frontier ideas, combined into one new product category.

We borrow public technical patterns, not proprietary technology.

A1

Autonomy Brain

Vision, world modeling, planning, efficient inference and fleet-learning patterns inspired by modern autonomy systems.

Reference: Tesla public AI & Robotics materials
A2

Intuitive Robot Interface

Robot-agnostic abstraction and human-first interaction so the copilot feels obvious instead of requiring robotics expertise.

Reference: Enigma public technology materials
A3

Dexterous Physical AI

Vision-language-action reasoning, simulation and manipulation data for kitchen, cleaning and cabin tasks.

Reference: Genesis AI public GENE materials
A4

Useful Work First

Constrained real-world tasks, human fallback and continuous improvement before attempting unrestricted autonomy.

Reference: Walden Robotics public deployment philosophy
