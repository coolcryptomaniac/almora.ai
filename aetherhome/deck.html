INVESTOR DECK / CONCEPT STAGE

Your car becomes home.
Your home learns to move.

AETHERHOME combines autonomous mobility, an adaptive premium cabin and a near-human embodied copilot into one autonomous living platform.

01 / CATEGORY

The autonomous living vehicle.

Not a better car. Not a robot accessory. A new product category that turns time in motion into usable life.

02 / PROBLEM

Mobility still consumes life.

Even as vehicles automate, the cabin remains mostly passive. Travelers still prepare, pack, clean, coordinate, eat, work and reset around the vehicle rather than having the vehicle orchestrate those tasks.

TimeTransit hours remain underutilized.
FrictionEvery arrival and departure requires repeated setup.
FragmentationCar, home, assistant and hospitality systems do not share one physical context.
03 / PRODUCT

One system that drives, hosts and resets.

MOTION OSAutonomous navigation and safety.
LIVING OSAdaptive cabin modes and personal environment.
ATLASEmbodied service and manipulation.
04 / EXPERIENCE

Work. Dine. Rest. Travel. Arrive ready.

Users should experience the product as a continuous space rather than a sequence of transportation events.

05 / WHY NOW

Three curves are converging.

AutonomyVehicle perception and planning are advancing rapidly.
Physical AIRobotics foundation models and dexterous manipulation are moving from research to useful work.
Premium mobilityConsumers and businesses already pay for time, privacy and convenience.
06 / WEDGE

Luxury fleet first.

Executive mobility, hotels, resorts, events and premium travel provide controlled environments and high willingness to pay. We sell the experience before attempting a clean-sheet mass-market vehicle.

07 / TECHNICAL PATH

Useful chores before general intelligence.

ATLAS begins with luggage, object retrieval, serving, tidying, folding and cabin preparation. Teleoperation fallback protects reliability while the autonomy dataset compounds.

08 / MOAT

The moving-home dataset.

In-cabin physical intelligence produces a rare dataset across manipulation, human proximity, preference, moving-platform dynamics, task recovery and environment orchestration.

09 / BUSINESS MODEL

Mobility + software + physical service.

Fleet economicsLease, hourly or usage-based premium mobility.
SoftwareLiving OS and fleet orchestration subscriptions.
ServiceEmbodied task modules, teleoperation and uptime.
10 / MILESTONES

Earn the right to raise bigger.

1) full-scale cabin demonstrator → 2) reliable constrained copilot benchmark → 3) paid premium pilot → 4) multi-vehicle learning loop → 5) partner-platform scale.

11 / CAPITAL

$5M proves the category.
$100M is earned by proof.

The public funding meter tracks verified, non-binding indications of interest. It is not a claim that a financing round has closed.

12 / VISION

The operating system for life in motion.

As autonomy and physical AI mature, the winning product may not be the car or the humanoid alone. It may be the system that makes an intelligent moving environment feel inevitable.
